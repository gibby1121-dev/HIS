## Supply notes

This intersection — **S780, ≤1,000 eng / ≤600 sep, AND first-owner** — is a
genuinely tight pool, for a structural reason. A 2–3-year-old S780 under 1,000
engine hours is a 1-crop machine worth $400K–$600K, and farmers with iron that
clean almost never sell it privately. It moves one of two ways: **(a) traded to
a Deere dealer** — which is why nearly every clean low-hour unit indexes as
dealer stock (Tier B) — or **(b) sold at a farm retirement/estate auction**
(Tier A). **Craigslist and Facebook Marketplace are effectively empty for this
class** — a $400K combine does not trade there; that is not a search gap.

The realistic play: if "by owner" is firm, this is an auction-and-classifieds
hunt with a wait — and the 2026 fall retirement-sale calendar (Bunnell 8/4,
Flaharty 8/18, plus rolling BigIron/Steffes/Sullivan consignments) is exactly
where owner units land. If "clean one-owner machine, source flexible" is close
enough, **B1 (Grundy Center, ~70 mi, in-spec, $419,900) is the machine, today.**
